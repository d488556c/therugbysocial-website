Help with Sunrise Alarm & Sleep Sounds

Need a hand? Email sunrisealarm.help@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within two business days. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Important: Sunrise Alarm is a gentle wake-up tool, not a guaranteed alarm. It depends on your phone being charged, your volume being up and iOS working normally. For anything you can’t miss, set a second alarm too. See our Terms of Use.

Getting started

  1. Set an alarm. Pick your wake-up time and how long the sunrise lasts.
  2. Tap Enter Sleep Mode at bedtime. Your sleep sound starts and the screen goes dark.
  3. Plug in and sleep. Leave the app open on your nightstand and wake up to the sunrise.

Common questions

How does the free trial work?

You can try everything free for 7 days. Nothing is charged when the trial starts or ends. Unlock the app forever with a one-time purchase: $1.99 during your trial, or $4.99 after it ends (US prices). There’s no subscription and no annual fee. If you don’t unlock it, the app simply locks when the trial ends.

Will I be charged automatically when the trial ends?

No. Sunrise Alarm never charges you automatically. You only pay if you tap Unlock and confirm the purchase with Apple. There’s nothing to cancel.

I got a new phone or reinstalled the app. How do I get my unlock back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Restore Purchases (at the bottom of the unlock screen, or under the ? button).

Do I need to leave the app open overnight?

Yes, for the full sunrise. The gradual light, sound and vibration only run while the app is open in Sleep Mode. Keep your phone plugged in. If the app gets closed, a backup alarm still rings shortly after your wake-up time.

I couldn’t hear my wake-up sound

The app uses your iPhone’s media volume. Turn your volume up before bed (the app reminds you if it’s low). The sound plays even when the ring/silent switch is set to silent.

Will my alarm still go off if the app is closed or my phone restarts?

Yes. On iOS 26 and later, the app sets a real system alarm as a backup. Allow alarms when the app asks, or turn them on later in Settings › Sunrise Alarm. On older iOS versions the backup is a notification with sound, which does not ring through silent mode.

The screen isn’t getting bright, or the flashlight isn’t turning on

Make sure the app is open and the phone isn’t locked. The flashlight is optional: turn on “Use flashlight” in the alarm’s settings and allow camera access when asked (it’s only used to control the flashlight). It joins in partway through the sunrise.

How do I snooze or stop the alarm?

Tap Snooze for 9 more minutes, followed by a short 1-minute sunrise, or tap Stop when you’re up. Alarms that don’t repeat turn themselves off after they ring.

Can I try the sunrise without waiting until morning?

Yes. On the home screen, tap “Try a 1-min sunrise” in the Gentle Rise card.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple, so we can’t issue refunds ourselves. You can request one from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
