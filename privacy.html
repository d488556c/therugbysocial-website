Privacy Policy

Sunrise Alarm & Sleep Sounds · Effective October 19, 2026

Sunrise Alarm & Sleep Sounds (“the app”) is made by The Rugby Social LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: the app doesn’t collect any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or anyone else.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

Your alarms and settings (alarm times, repeat days, sounds, sunrise length, light color and similar preferences) are saved only on your iPhone so the app can work. They are not shared with us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Alarms and notifications: to set a backup alarm, so you still wake up if the app has been closed.
  • Camera: only to turn on the flashlight during the sunrise, if you choose that option. The app never takes photos or video and never accesses your camera’s images.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your iPhone’s Settings app.

Purchases

If you buy the app, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We don’t receive your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
